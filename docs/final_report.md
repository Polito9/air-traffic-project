## 1. Objective and project phases

We wanted to analyse European air traffic at scale with ADS-B data from the OpenSky Network: detect holding patterns, measure congestion in terminal areas (TMAs), compare traffic across periods, and show the results on a map. We built an end-to-end big data pipeline (Docker Compose: MinIO, Kafka, Spark, MongoDB) and split the work in five phases:

1. **Infrastructure:** containers, MinIO buckets, MongoDB collections with `2dsphere` indexes.
2. **Ingestion:** live OpenSky states into Kafka; historical files in MinIO; TMA polygons in MongoDB.
3. **Cleaning and flight grouping:** Spark filters noisy pings and builds GeoJSON `LineString` trajectories.
4. **Spatial analytics:** holding patterns, TMA congestion (`$geoIntersects`), time of day, historical comparison.
5. **Visualization:** Streamlit + Kepler.gl dashboard.

The repository layout and run instructions are in `README.md`.

## 2. Dataset

| Dataset | Source | Size | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Live state vectors | OpenSky REST `/states/all`, Europe (lat 35–62, lon −12–30), one snapshot every 15–30 s, OAuth2 client | 1,097 snapshots, **2,281,579** distinct state vectors, 10,440 aircraft | **Real** |
| Flight lists, April 2019 / 2020 / 2022 | OpenSky flight lists (Zenodo, record 7923702): origin, destination, times of every flight | 3 monthly files (≈420 MB), 630 airport/day rows | **Real** |
| Simulated trajectories 2019, 2020, 2023 | Generated by `ingest_historical.py` | 137 flights, 3,568 rows | **Simulated** |

The live capture ran in two blocks (29 Oct 19:34 – 30 Oct 01:47 UTC and 30 Oct 08:26 – 12:40 UTC, about 10.5 h; gaps at night and in the afternoon limit day-level conclusions). It is regenerated by `stream_opensky_kafka_oauth.py`. OpenSky's Trino access was not granted, so the simulated trajectories (6 hub airports, injected noise and holding loops) only validate the pipeline; every row is labelled `dataset=synthetic_<year>` and no conclusion is drawn from them. Real historical evidence comes from the public flight lists, which contain flights but not trajectories.

## 3. Methodology

- **Infrastructure (Docker Compose, stock images):** MinIO (buckets `airtraffic-raw`, `airtraffic-processed`), Kafka + Zookeeper, Spark 3.5 (master + worker), MongoDB 7 (`airtraffic_db`, `2dsphere` indexes), created by `setup_infra.py`.
- **Landing (Spark, 3a):** reads the whole Kafka topic, explodes each payload into one row per aircraft state, de-duplicates on (`icao24`, time) and writes Parquet to MinIO.
- **Cleaning and sessionization (Spark, 3b):** rows with missing/invalid coordinates or speed above Mach 1 (343 m/s) are dropped. Per aircraft, a window function (`lag` plus a cumulative sum) starts a new flight session after a gap longer than 30 minutes, and consecutive repeated positions are removed. Each session (≥ 2 points) becomes a GeoJSON `LineString` with per-point times, headings, speeds and altitudes in MongoDB (deterministic `_id`, idempotent reruns).
- **Holding detection (Spark, 4a):** a window over each session sums the signed heading change in a sliding 20-minute window. A holding is flagged when the sum reaches 360° inside a ~50 km box (speed ≥ 75 m/s, ≥ 6 pings, ≥ 2 min); consecutive hits are merged into one event in `holding_events`.
- **Congestion (MongoDB, 4b):** `$geoIntersects` finds the trajectories crossing each TMA polygon. Aircraft are then counted per 10-minute bucket only while their pings lie inside the area.
- **Time of day (4c):** distinct aircraft per 10-minute bucket, averaged per hour (UTC), and holdings per hour.
- **Historical comparison (Spark, 4d):** the monthly flight lists are uploaded to MinIO and read by Spark; arrivals (`day`) and departures (`firstseen`) are counted per day for the six airports, plus all European airports (ICAO prefix E/L), into `airport_daily`.
- **Dashboard (Phase 5):** Streamlit with Kepler.gl or pydeck: map, congestion charts, time-of-day and historical trends, holdings table.

## 4. Results

### 4.1 Cleaning and trajectories

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Rows in → clean (dropped: 3 invalid coordinates, 120 above Mach 1) | 2,285,147 → 2,285,024 |
| Trajectories (live / simulated) | 23,435 (23,298 / 137) |

Live sessions last 33 min on median (max 258 min); they are observed segments, not complete flights.

### 4.2 Holding patterns

160 events from 127 aircraft (median duration 8.8 min). Many are military, training or surveillance orbits next to a few commercial holds, because we do not filter by operator.

### 4.3 Time of day (real data, UTC)

| Window (UTC) | Aircraft per 10 min (hourly average) | Holdings started |
|---|---|---|
| 08:00 – 12:59 (Fri) | 2,550 – 3,950 | 138 |
| 19:00 – 23:59 (Thu) | 790 – 1,620 | 21 |
| 00:00 – 01:59 (Fri) | 340 – 470 | 1 |

The late-morning peak has about 11× the traffic of the night, and 138 of the 160 holdings (86%) start between 08:00 and 12:59. Only 12 hours (a Thursday evening and a Friday morning) are covered, so this is a descriptive profile, not a tested effect.

### 4.4 Congestion per TMA (live data)

| TMA (60 km area) | London | Paris | Frankfurt | Amsterdam | Madrid | Munich |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Flights crossing it | 1,994 | 1,597 | 1,136 | 916 | 910 | 866 |
| Peak aircraft per 10 min | 196 | 123 | 85 | 73 | 67 | 66 |

London is the busiest TMA, followed by Paris and Frankfurt; the peaks fall between 10:40 and 12:10 UTC.

### 4.5 Real historical comparison (April, arrivals)

| Arrivals in April | 2019 | 2020 | 2022 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 TMA airports (total) | 101,437 | 11,416 (−89%) | 91,499 (−10%) |
| All European airports | 517,751 | 77,049 (−85%) | 601,844 (+16%) |

The COVID-19 collapse is clear (April 2020 vs 2019: Munich and Madrid −94%, London −87%, Paris −89%, Frankfurt −81%, Amsterdam −88%). The +16% Europe-wide in 2022, while the six hubs are still below 2019, probably reflects the growth of OpenSky's receiver network, so absolute counts across years are indicative only.

## 5. Deviations from the proposal and limitations

| Item in the proposal | What we did and why |
|---|---|
| COVID-19 comparison (2019 vs 2020 vs 2023) | **Partly achieved with real data**: April 2019/2020/2022 flight lists (4.5), arrivals only. 2023 is not in that dataset and trajectory-level data were not granted, so trajectory comparisons across years are simulated and only validate the pipeline. |
| Arrival throughput, routes, diversions | Arrivals per airport are real only for the historical months (4.5). Diversions **not computed** (no planned destination). For live data, replaced by airborne density (distinct aircraft inside each TMA per 10 min). |
| Sessions, TMAs | Time-gap sessions only (30 min). TMAs are simplified 60 km circles, not official boundaries. |
