In this assignment, each group should select one research paper published within the last five years at the intersection of data-intensive platforms and societal or environmental issues. The assignment has two parts: (1) a written report, and (2) a 10–15 minute recorded video presentation.

The written report should be approximately two pages. It should briefly explain the main problem or problems discussed in the paper, describe the approach or solution proposed by the authors, and provide your own critical reflection. In your reflection, explain whether you agree or disagree with the paper’s arguments, approach, or conclusions, and give clear reasons for your position.

The video presentation should summarize the same points in a clear and concise way. It should introduce the paper, explain the main problem and proposed approach, and present your group’s critical reflection on the work.
