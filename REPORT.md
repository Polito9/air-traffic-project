## 1. Objective and project phases

We wanted to analyse European air traffic at scale with ADS-B data from the OpenSky Network: detect holding patterns, measure congestion in terminal areas (TMAs), compare traffic across periods, and show the results on a map. We built an end-to-end big data pipeline (Docker Compose: MinIO, Kafka, Spark, MongoDB) and split the work in five phases:

1. **Infrastructure:** containers, MinIO buckets, MongoDB collections with `2dsphere` indexes.
2. **Ingestion:** live OpenSky states into Kafka; historical files in MinIO; TMA polygons in MongoDB.
3. **Cleaning and flight grouping:** Spark filters noisy pings and builds GeoJSON `LineString` trajectories.
4. **Spatial analytics:** holding patterns, TMA congestion (`$geoIntersects`), time of day, historical comparison.
5. **Visualization:** Streamlit + Kepler.gl dashboard.

Repository layout and run instructions: [`README.md`](README.md).

## 2. Dataset

| Dataset | Source | Size | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Live state vectors | OpenSky REST API [`/states/all`](https://openskynetwork.github.io/opensky-api/rest.html), Europe (lat 35–62, lon −12–30), one snapshot every 15–30 s, OAuth2 | ≈1,600 snapshots, **3,831,206** distinct state vectors, 13,071 aircraft | **Real** |
| Flight lists, Feb–Jun 2019 and 2020, Apr 2022 | OpenSky flight lists ([Zenodo 7923702](https://zenodo.org/records/7923702)): origin, destination, times | 11 monthly files (≈1.6 GB), 2,317 airport/day rows | **Real** |
| Simulated trajectories 2019, 2020, 2023 | Generated by `ingest_historical.py` | 137 flights, 3,568 rows | **Simulated** |

The live capture ran in three blocks (29 Oct 19:34 – 30 Oct 01:47, 30 Oct 08:26 – 12:40 and 31 Oct 12:34 – 16:58 UTC, about 15 h, plus a few test snapshots at 15:06 UTC on 30 Oct; the gaps limit day-level conclusions). OpenSky's Trino access was not granted, so the simulated trajectories (6 hub airports, injected noise and holding loops) only validate the pipeline; every row is labelled `dataset=synthetic_<year>` and no conclusion is drawn from them. Real historical evidence comes from public flight lists (flights, not trajectories).

## 3. Methodology

- **Infrastructure (Docker Compose, stock images):** MinIO (buckets `airtraffic-raw`, `airtraffic-processed`), Kafka + Zookeeper, Spark 3.5 (master + worker), MongoDB 7 (`airtraffic_db`, `2dsphere` indexes), created by `setup_infra.py`.
- **Landing (Spark, 3a):** reads the Kafka topic, one row per aircraft state, de-duplicates on (`icao24`, time), writes Parquet to MinIO.
- **Cleaning and sessionization (Spark, 3b):** rows with missing/invalid coordinates or speed above Mach 1 (343 m/s) are dropped. Per aircraft, a window function (`lag` plus a cumulative sum) starts a new flight session after a gap longer than 30 minutes, and consecutive repeated positions are removed. Each session (≥ 2 points) becomes a GeoJSON `LineString` with per-point times, headings, speeds and altitudes in MongoDB (deterministic `_id`, idempotent reruns).
- **Holding detection (Spark, 4a):** a window over each session sums the signed heading change in a sliding 20-minute window. A holding is flagged when the sum reaches 360° inside a ~50 km box (speed ≥ 75 m/s, ≥ 6 pings, ≥ 2 min); consecutive hits are merged into one event in `holding_events`.
- **Congestion (MongoDB, 4b):** `$geoIntersects` finds the trajectories crossing each TMA polygon. Aircraft are then counted per 10-minute bucket only while their pings lie inside the area.
- **Time of day (4c):** distinct aircraft per 10-minute bucket, averaged per hour (UTC), and holdings per hour.
- **Historical comparison (Spark, 4d):** flight lists uploaded to MinIO; Spark counts daily arrivals (`day`) and departures (`firstseen`) at the six airports and all European airports (ICAO prefix E/L) into `airport_daily`.
- **Dashboard (Phase 5):** Streamlit (Kepler.gl or pydeck): map, congestion, time-of-day and historical trends, holdings.

## 4. Results

### 4.1 Cleaning and trajectories

3,834,774 rows in, 3,834,587 clean (3 invalid coordinates and 184 above Mach 1 dropped). 36,421 trajectories (36,284 live, 137 simulated); live sessions last 42 min on median (max 266 min) and are observed segments, not complete flights.

### 4.2 Holding patterns

186 events from 148 aircraft (median duration 8.7 min). 27 (15%) are inside the London TMA, with airline callsigns (BAW, AAL, ACA, ASA), none inside the other five. The other 159 lie outside our circles and were not classified (we do not filter by operator; many look military or training orbits).

### 4.3 Time of day (real data, UTC)

| Window (UTC) | Aircraft per 10 min (hourly average) | Holdings started |
|---|---|---|
| 08:00 – 12:59 (Fri) | 2,550 – 3,950 | 138 |
| 19:00 – 23:59 (Thu) | 790 – 1,620 | 21 |
| 00:00 – 01:59 (Fri) | 340 – 470 | 1 |
| 12:00 – 16:59 (Sat) | 2,280 – 3,780 | 26 |

The late-morning peak has about 11× the traffic of the night, and 138 of the 186 holdings (74%) start on Friday between 08:00 and 12:59. Saturday afternoon has similar traffic but only 3–6 holdings per hour (Friday morning: 19–45). Only 17 hourly slots are covered: descriptive, not a tested effect.

### 4.4 Congestion per TMA (live data)

| TMA (60 km area) | London | Paris | Frankfurt | Amsterdam | Madrid | Munich |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Flights crossing it | 3,492 | 2,718 | 1,952 | 1,506 | 1,500 | 1,499 |
| Peak aircraft per 10 min | 196 | 123 | 87 | 73 | 67 | 69 |

London is the busiest TMA, then Paris and Frankfurt; peaks fall between 10:40 and 14:40 UTC.

The COVID-19 collapse starts in March, bottoms out in April (Munich and Madrid −94%) and recovers slowly; February 2020 is higher partly because of the leap day and probably a larger receiver network. April 2022: 91,499 arrivals at the hubs (−10% vs 2019), 601,844 Europe-wide (+16%), probably receiver growth: absolute counts across years are indicative only.

## 5. Deviations from the proposal and limitations

| Item in the proposal | What we did and why |
|---|---|
| COVID-19 comparison (2019 vs 2020 vs 2023) | **Partly achieved with real data**: Feb–Jun 2019/2020 and Apr 2022 flight lists (4.5), arrivals only. No 2023 in that dataset; trajectories across years are simulated (pipeline validation only). |
| Arrivals, routes, diversions | Real arrivals only for the historical months (4.5); diversions **not computed**; live data use airborne density per TMA. |
| Sessions, TMAs | Time-gap sessions only; TMAs are simplified 60 km circles. |
